IN THREE STEPS

## A shopping decision *that knows you.*

Every brand’s fit is different.
Let your own measurements always be your starting point.

01 / MEASUREMENTS

### Two photos or a tape measure.

We estimate your body measurements from two photos you take from the front and the side. You see every measurement; if you have a value you measured with a tape, you can enter it. A measurement you enter yourself always takes priority.

02 / ITEM

### Enter the item’s size chart.

You enter the chest, waist and hip measurements from the size chart on the store’s product page into the app. You can also choose the item’s fit and how stretchy the fabric is.

03 / RECOMMENDATION

### Not just a size, but the reason.

You see the recommended size and how each size would sit at the chest, waist and hips (tight, good fit, loose), one by one. The final decision is always yours.

SEE WHAT’S BEHIND THE RECOMMENDATION

## More than *“get an M.”*

Switch sizes and see how the sample fit explanation changes. Kabin’s aim isn’t just to give you a letter, but to make your decision easier to understand.

This interaction uses fixed, illustrative data. It doesn’t take any measurements, doesn’t send data anywhere and isn’t a real purchase recommendation.

PHOTO MEASUREMENTS

## Two photos, *your measurements.*

No tape measure to hand? Start with your photos, and refine your measurements with a tape whenever you like.

### How do I take them?

Two full-length photos, one from the front and one from the side. You also enter your height, weight and age, which support the estimate. The silhouette guide in the app shows you how to stand.

### What do I get?

Estimated values for your body measurements. If you add a value you measured with a tape, the recommendation always uses your own measurement.

### What happens to my photos?

They are processed only to estimate your measurements, in our own server’s memory for the duration of the analysis. They are not written to disk or to logs, are not stored and are never sent to any third-party AI service.

YOUR QUESTIONS

## A little closer.

### How accurate are measurements from photos?

Measurements from photos are estimates. We don’t yet publish a validated error rate. Loose clothing, a tilted posture or poor lighting affect the result; for the most precise result, you can check your measurements with a tape measure and correct them.

### Are my photos stored?

No. Photos are processed only in our server’s memory for the duration of the analysis; they are not written to disk or to logs, and they are not sent to third-party AI services. The measurement values from the analysis are stored on your device and in the measurement profile tied to your account.

### How reliable is the size recommendation?

Recommendations are estimates. The result depends on how accurate your measurements are, how accurate the store’s size chart is, and the fit information. Kabin does not guarantee fit.

### How do I delete my data?

You can delete your account and all data linked to it from the account settings in the app. For other requests, you can reach us through the channels listed in the [KVKK information notice](https://kabincommunity.com/yasal/kvkk-aydinlatma.html#haklar) (in Turkish).
