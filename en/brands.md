# We’ll tell them their size.

Your customer enters their measurements on your product page and sees their size.

[Ask about a pilot](#pilot) [See how it looks](#store)

## Here it is in your store.

Your page gets one button. The rest happens right there, and the customer never leaves the page.

Example page.

## A pilot in three steps.

1. **We look at your size chart.** Together we choose which items to try it on.
1. **We turn the button on for those items.** Your customer taps the button and sees their size.
1. **We look at the results together.**

In the pilot we measure returns and sales together. You see the result in your own numbers.

Only your customer sees the measurements. You get the suggested size.
