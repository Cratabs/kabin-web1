ABOUT

Why are we building Kabin?

Every brand cuts differently. The same M is tight at one brand and loose at another. Kabin goes by your measurements, not the letter on the label.

What do we do?

  • Measurements: We take your measurements with a tape measure or from two photos, front and side. What you enter from the tape always comes first.
  • Size: We look at the item’s size chart and tell you your size. We also show how it will sit at the chest, waist and hips.
  • Wardrobe: You add your clothes and put outfits together. Kabin scores how well the pieces go together.
  • Community: You share your outfits and look at other people’s.

What happens to your data?

We don’t keep your body photos. We don’t sell your data or use it for ads. The details are in our Privacy Policy (in Turkish).
