ABOUT US

# Why are we building Kabin?

Every brand’s fit is different; the same “M” can be tight at one brand and loose at another. Kabin set out to free size choice from the letter on the label and base it on your measurements instead.

## What do we do?

- **Photo measurements:** we estimate your body measurements from two photos, one from the front and one from the side; values you measure with a tape always take priority.
- **Explained size recommendations:** we compare the item’s size chart with your measurements and show how each size would sit at the chest, waist and hips.
- **Wardrobe and outfits:** you add your pieces and put outfits together; our model scores how well the pieces go together.
- **Community:** you share your outfits and get inspired by other people’s style.

## How do we work?

Measurement estimates, size recommendations and outfit scores are calculated with models running on our own servers. Body photos are not stored and are never sent to any third-party AI service. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising. The details are in our [Privacy Policy](https://kabincommunity.com/yasal/gizlilik.html) (in Turkish).
