KABIN / FOR BRANDS

Reduce size uncertainty.
Strengthen the moment of decision.

Help your customers answer “Which size?” with more confidence. Kabin matches the measurement profile a user creates from photos or with a tape measure against your product’s size chart and fit information, and explains the reasoning behind its recommendation area by area. Let’s validate this approach together in your brand’s shopping experience.

LET’S VALIDATE THE VALUE TOGETHER

One integration.
Three clear research questions.

What is the effect on returns?

We aim to measure the rate of size-related returns, together with return reasons and a comparable control group.

Does it make deciding easier?

Let’s track together the purchase decisions of users who see the recommendation, and their feedback on choosing the right size.

Does it suit your team?

Let’s assess the adequacy of your product measurement data, the integration effort and the service latency throughout the pilot.

START SMALL, PROVE IT

How does the pilot work?

Let’s agree on the scope and success criteria together, before seeing any results.

01 / PREPARATION

Data and scope check

Product category, size chart, integration permissions and personal data roles are clarified; a confidentiality and data processing agreement is signed.

02 / LIMITED TRIAL

Controlled integration

The test plan is carried out on selected products, with sufficient data and clear information for users.

03 / EVALUATION

A decision based on results

Recommendation accuracy, service performance and commercial results are assessed together; the decision to scale is based on evidence.
