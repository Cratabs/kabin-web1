QUESTIONS

Questions

How accurate are measurements from photos?

Measurements from photos are estimates. We don’t yet publish a validated error rate. Loose clothing, a tilted posture or poor lighting affect the result; for the most precise result, you can check your measurements with a tape measure and correct them.

Are my photos stored?

No. Photos are processed only in our server’s memory for the duration of the analysis; they are not written to disk or to logs, and they are not sent to third-party AI services. The measurement values from the analysis are stored on your device and in the measurement profile tied to your account.

How reliable is the size recommendation?

Recommendations are estimates. The result depends on how accurate your measurements are, how accurate the store’s size chart is, and the fit information. Kabin does not guarantee fit.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete your account and all data linked to it from the account settings in the app. For other requests, you can reach us through the channels listed in the KVKK information notice (in Turkish).
